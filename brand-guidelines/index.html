The logo.

It has been on the sack since 1969. We use it exactly as it was drawn.

  • Never redraw, stretch, tilt or recolour it. Use the supplied files only.
  • Give it room. Keep clear space equal to the height of the word "Rice" on every side.
  • Full colour on white. The white version goes on Royal Indigo.
  • Smallest size: 120 px or 24 mm wide for the full logo. Below that, use the shield.
  • On photographs, place it over a calm, even area, never over faces or busy detail.
Clear space = one "Rice"

Craft and shape.

The hand-made details, and the one rule about shape.

  • Sharp rectangles. Photographs, product tiles and panels have square corners.
  • No arches or rounded frames, except the single "given time" device.
  • Pills are for buttons only.
  • One flat colour for bands, frames, icons and the seal. No gradients or shadows on them.

Warli bands

Three friezes (harvest, kitchen, dance) run between sections, white on indigo or indigo on white.

The seal

For provenance and sign-offs. Always on indigo.

Heading.

Frame and stitch

A thin Warli frame on product tiles, a stitch under headings. Always red.

The print

Story photographs are laid down like album prints, slightly tilted.

Icons and pattern

Block-print icons in one colour; the same shapes, faint, as pattern.
